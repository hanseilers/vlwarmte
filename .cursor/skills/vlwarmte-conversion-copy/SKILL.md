# VLWarmte Conversion Copy System

Use this skill when writing or revising copy on `index.html`, `contact.html`, `prijsindicatie.html`, city pages, and Ads-aligned landing content.

## Voice constraints

- Noord-Nederlands: direct, calm, competent.
- Avoid hype and superlatives.
- Be specific about process, timelines, and outcomes.
- Reinforce "10 jaar garantie" on buis where relevant.

## Funnel-aware copy rules

### Cold intent (first visit)
- Lead with concrete outcome and service scope.
- Add one trust anchor early (region, trajectory ownership, response expectation).
- CTA: low-friction ("Bekijk aanpak", "Vraag richtbedrag").

### Warm intent (comparison)
- Clarify fit and boundaries (wat wel/niet in traject).
- Add proof snippets (project type, planning reliability, workmanship cues).
- CTA: medium-friction ("Plan offertegesprek", "Bekijk systemen").

### Hot intent (ready to act)
- Remove ambiguity on next step and response time.
- State what happens after submit in 1-2 lines.
- CTA: high-intent ("Offerte aanvragen", "Bel mij terug").

## CTA patterns

- Primary CTA should map to one intent at a time.
- Avoid conflicting primaries in one viewport unless one is clearly dominant.
- Contact form CTA must reflect user mode (`informatie`, `offerte`, `bel`).

## Copy quality checks

- Every section answers: "Wat levert dit mij op?"
- Replace abstract claims with specifics.
- Prefer numbers when available (timeline, region, guarantee duration).
- Keep jargon low unless immediately explained.

## Output format

When applied, return:

1. Before -> after copy suggestions per section.
2. Rationale tied to funnel stage.
3. Risks (legal, ambiguity, mismatch with actual service scope).
4. Final recommended copy snippets ready to paste.
